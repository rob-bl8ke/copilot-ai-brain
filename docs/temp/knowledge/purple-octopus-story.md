# The Legend of the Purple Octopus

In the quiet seaside village of Wobbleton lived a mysterious creature known as **Octavius the Purple Octopus**.

Octavius was no ordinary octopus.

He wore a tiny golden monocle and spent his evenings solving riddles while drinking seaweed tea.

His best friends were:

• **Gerald the Forgetful Pelican** – a bird who could never remember where he parked his bicycle.

• **Madame Bubbles** – a jellyfish who ran the town's underwater library.

• **Sir Clanksalot** – a rusty crab knight who guarded the lighthouse.

One day Octavius discovered an ancient treasure map hidden inside a sardine tin.

The map led to a legendary artifact known as **The Infinite Teapot of Biscuits**, said to produce endless tea biscuits.

But the treasure could only be claimed by answering three riddles carved into a coral arch.

The riddles were:

1. What walks on waves but never gets wet?
2. What has eight arms but hugs no one?
3. What pours forever but is never empty?

Only Octavius knew the answers.
