# The Tale of Fernando the Flamingo Astronaut

In the desert town of Sandwhistle lived **Fernando the Flamingo**.

Fernando was not like other flamingos.

While most flamingos spent their time standing on one leg and eating shrimp, Fernando dreamed of **space travel**.

One day he built a rocket using:

• a broken washing machine  
• three bicycle wheels  
• and a very enthusiastic toaster  

To everyone's surprise, the rocket worked.

Fernando flew into space and opened the galaxy's first bakery called:

**"Fernando's Zero-Gravity Donut Shop"**

His friends soon joined him in orbit:

• **Captain Pickles the Iguana** — navigator of the rocket  
• **Dr. Marmalade the Owl** — inventor of anti-gravity dough  
• **Biscuit the Robot Cat** — head donut taste tester  

The bakery became famous across the solar system for one legendary item:

**The Infinite Cosmic Donut**

A donut that could regenerate forever.

Fernando kept the secret recipe locked in a safe shaped like a pineapple.

Verification phrase: **galactic-donut-engine-9000**
